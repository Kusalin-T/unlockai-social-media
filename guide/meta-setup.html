Get your Instagram key,
step by step

ค่อย ๆ ทำทีละขั้น เดี๋ยวได้ API key ของ Instagram มาแน่นอน — มีทีมงานช่วยตรงนี้
→Create your own Meta app (free)
→Copy your App ID + App Secret (the "secret key")
→Generate your access token (the "API key")
→Publish the app so it works on real followers
◆Take it slow — one screen per step. Stuck on any screen? Raise your hand, a helper will jump in. ติดตรงไหนยกมือได้เลย
❯ step 1 · create the app

Go to the Meta developer site and create an app

เข้าเว็บนักพัฒนาของ Meta แล้วสร้างแอปใหม่
screenshotdevelopers.facebook.com — the "Create App" button (top right)screens/01-create-app.png
DO THIS
Open developers.facebook.com → log in with your Facebook account → click Create App.
  • Use the same login that your Instagram is connected to.
  • If it asks you to register as a developer first, accept and continue — it's free.
❯ step 2 · choose the use case

Pick "Other" → then the Business type

เลือก Other แล้วเลือกประเภท Business
screenshotThe use-case picker — choose "Other", then app type "Business"screens/02-usecase.png
DO THIS
Choose Other at the bottom → Next → app type Business → Next.
  • Give it any name (e.g. "my-comment-bot"). Only you see it.
  • Pick your business account if it asks; otherwise leave it as "No business portfolio".
❯ step 3 · the one trap to avoid

You land on the dashboard. Don't click "Become a Tech Provider"

พอเข้า dashboard แล้ว อย่ากดปุ่ม "Become a Tech Provider" เด็ดขาด
screenshotThe app dashboard — circle/point at "Become a Tech Provider" with a red "don't click" markscreens/03-dashboard-no-techprovider.png
WHY IT MATTERS
"Become a Tech Provider" starts the App Review process — which you do not need for your own account.
▲For your own Instagram, no App Review is required. Skip that button and keep going.
❯ step 4 · add Instagram

Add the Instagram product → "API setup with Instagram login"

เพิ่มผลิตภัณฑ์ Instagram แล้วเลือก "API setup with Instagram login"
screenshotProducts list → Instagram "Set up" → the "API setup with Instagram login" optionscreens/04-add-instagram.png
DO THIS
In the products list, find Instagram → Set up → choose API setup with Instagram login.
◆This path needs no Facebook Page — it works straight off your Instagram. ไม่ต้องมีเพจ Facebook
❯ step 5 · your secret key

Copy your App ID and App Secret

คัดลอก App ID และ App Secret เก็บไว้ — นี่คือ "secret key"
screenshotApp settings → Basic → App ID and App Secret (blur the secret value in the shot)screens/05-app-id-secret.png
DO THIS
Left sidebar → App settings → Basic. Copy the App ID and click Show on App Secret.

▲Treat the App Secret like a password — paste it only where the assistant tells you, never in a public post or chat.
❯ step 6 · connect your Instagram

Add your Instagram as a tester, and accept in the app

เพิ่มบัญชี Instagram ของคุณเป็น tester แล้วกดยอมรับในแอป Instagram
screenshotRoles / Instagram testers → add your IG username; + the "accept" screen inside the IG app (Settings → Apps and websites → Tester invites)screens/06-add-tester.png
DO THIS
Add your IG handle as an Instagram Tester, then open Instagram on your phone → Settings → Apps and websites → Tester invites → Accept.
  • Your Instagram must be a Professional account (Business or Creator).
❯ step 7 · your API key (token)

Generate your access token

สร้าง access token — นี่คือ "API key" ที่ระบบใช้ทำงานแทนคุณ
screenshotThe "Generate access token" button + the token result (blur the token value)screens/07-generate-token.png
DO THIS
On the Instagram API setup page → Generate access token → approve the permission screen → copy the long token.
Access tokenIGQV…
◆This token lasts ~60 days; the assistant will help you refresh it later. โทเคนอยู่ได้ ~60 วัน เดี๋ยวช่วยต่ออายุให้
❯ step 8 · privacy policy URL

Add a Privacy Policy URL (needed to publish)

ใส่ลิงก์ Privacy Policy — จำเป็นก่อนกด publish
screenshotApp settings → Basic → the Privacy Policy URL field filled inscreens/08-privacy-url.png
DO THIS
App settings → Basic → paste a real, reachable Privacy Policy URL → Save Changes.
  • Don't have one? The assistant can publish a 2-minute policy page for you — just ask.
❯ step 9 · go live

Publish the app — this is what makes it work on real followers

กด Publish — ขั้นนี้แหละที่ทำให้บอทเห็นคอมเมนต์ของคนจริง ๆ
screenshotLeft sidebar → Publish, showing the "Unpublished" badge flipping after publishingscreens/09-publish.png
DO THIS
Left sidebar → Publish → confirm. The Unpublished badge disappears.
▲Skip this and the bot sees zero comments from normal followers. This step is the usual "why isn't it working".
❯ step 10 · hand the keys to the assistant

Paste your three values where the assistant asks

เอา 3 ค่านี้ไปวางตรงที่ผู้ช่วยบอก แล้วมันจะตั้งค่าให้เอง
screenshotTerminal/Claude asking for the keys, values pasted (blurred) — optionalscreens/10-paste-keys.png

Back in Claude, tell it you have your keys. It will save them safely and wire up the bot.

❯ done · test it for real

Test it — the right way

ทดสอบให้ถูกวิธี: ให้เพื่อนคอมเมนต์ ไม่ใช่คอมเมนต์เอง
Have the person next to you comment your keyword under your post — from their account.
  • You should get a public reply under the comment and an auto-DM to them.
  • You can't test from your own account (own comments are skipped, and you can't DM yourself).
  • The DM lands in their Requests folder if they don't follow you yet — that's normal.
✓Both the reply and the DM showed up? You're live. ขึ้นทั้งคู่ = ใช้งานได้จริงแล้ว 🎉
▲Never re-send a DM that "looks failed" — it may have already been delivered. One comment = one DM. Let the assistant handle sends.
